# About the Agentic System

**BUS 4498 Team Build**

> **Problem to be solved**: Orfalea College of Business advising appointments go unused at a high rate during the registration window. Students book a slot weeks in advance, forget about it, and never cancel. The advisor sits idle while other students are told nothing is open for two weeks. Our rough baseline is a 25% no-show rate on advising appointments in the month surrounding registration, and almost none of those abandoned slots get released back to the booking queue.

### System Name

SlotSaver

### System Goal

- **Beneficiary**: Orfalea undergraduates who need an advising appointment before their registration date, and the advisors whose booked hours currently go unused.
- **Outcome**: Booked advising slots are either attended or released early enough that another student can claim them.
- **Measure**: Raise the attend-to-booking rate from a baseline of 75% to a target of 90%. Reopen at least 80% of cancelled slots to public booking within one hour of cancellation.
- **Boundary**: Covers undergraduate Orfalea advising appointments booked through the existing scheduling system during the four weeks around each registration period. The agent sends reminders, processes confirmations and cancellations, and reopens slots. It does not reschedule a student without their confirmation, and it does not advise on course selection.

### Who Is Better Off When This Works?

Orfalea students who currently cannot find an advising appointment before registration opens are better off, because abandoned slots get reopened fast enough for someone else to claim them.
